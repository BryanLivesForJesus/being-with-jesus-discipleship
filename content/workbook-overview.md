## Core Discipleship Framework
1. **Jesus Changes Everything:** He continues to change human history and our lives.
2. **Live Differently:** We are apprentices to Jesus, living in step with Him, shaped by His way rather than the world's (Spiritual Formation).
3. **Send It:** Jesus sends us out into His mission to serve others, share His message, and sacrifice.

## Daily Study Rhythm & Structure
* **Cadence:** 5 days a week, ~20 minutes per day.
* **Daily 4-Part Format:**
  1. **Read:** Assigned daily scripture passage.
  2. **Think:** Observation and reflection questions (what stood out, what questions arose, what was learned about Jesus).
  3. **Live Differently:** Personal application challenge.
  4. **Pray:** Directed closing prayer time.
